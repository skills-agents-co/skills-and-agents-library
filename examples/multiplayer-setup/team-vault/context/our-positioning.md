# Our positioning

The competitor-intel skill reads this to ground the "strengths vs. us" and "gaps we
beat them on" sections. Replace the placeholders with your real product. Keep it short
and current.

## What we sell

One-line description of our product and who it is for.

## Where we are strong

- The two or three things we genuinely do better than the field.
- Be specific (a capability, a price point, an integration), not "great support".

## Where we are weak

- The places competitors beat us today. Naming these makes the briefs honest and useful
  in a sales call.

## Our pricing, in one line

The tiers and prices we compare against, so a competitor's pricing can be read relative
to ours.
