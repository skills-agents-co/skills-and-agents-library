# Sample theses

Freeform cross-cutting interest notes for the sample entity folder. `news-monitor` reads this file, if
present, to shape relevance ranking — it does not change matching.

- I care most about anything touching robotics hardware supply chains or new funding rounds, since
  that's where the tracked organizations sit.
- Hiring and leadership moves at tracked people's current companies matter more to me than general
  press mentions.
