# State Unemployment Agencies

For each state: official agency, file-a-claim URL, claimant phone, and notes worth flagging. URLs and phones change occasionally — if a link looks dead, point the user to [CareerOneStop](https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/UnemploymentBenefits/find-unemployment-benefits.aspx) which the US Department of Labor keeps current.

Waiting period column: "1 wk" means the first eligible week is unpaid. "None" means benefits start week 1. This is the most common state-specific variable.

---

## Alabama
- **Agency:** Alabama Department of Labor
- **File:** https://labor.alabama.gov/uc/
- **Phone:** 1-800-361-4524
- **Waiting period:** 1 wk
- **Notes:** Must register for work at AlabamaWorks.com. Work search: 3 contacts/week.

## Alaska
- **Agency:** Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development
- **File:** https://my.alaska.gov/ (then UI Online)
- **Phone:** 1-888-252-2557
- **Waiting period:** 1 wk
- **Notes:** Files biweekly, not weekly. Dependent allowance available ($24/dependent/week, up to 3).

## Arizona
- **Agency:** Arizona Department of Economic Security (DES)
- **File:** https://des.az.gov/services/employment/unemployment-individual
- **Phone:** 1-877-600-2722
- **Waiting period:** 1 wk
- **Notes:** Max benefit is one of the lowest in the country (~$320/wk as of recent updates). Work search: 4 contacts/week.

## Arkansas
- **Agency:** Arkansas Division of Workforce Services
- **File:** https://www.ezarc.adws.arkansas.gov/
- **Phone:** 1-844-908-2178
- **Waiting period:** 1 wk
- **Notes:** Must register at Arkansas JobLink within 7 days.

## California
- **Agency:** Employment Development Department (EDD)
- **File:** https://edd.ca.gov/en/Unemployment/
- **Phone:** 1-800-300-5616
- **Waiting period:** 1 wk
- **Notes:** Use UI Online via myEDD account. ID.me identity verification required for most claimants. Phone wait times are notoriously long — exhaust the portal first. Certify every two weeks. Has the highest max weekly benefit on the West Coast but processing delays are common — file the day you're separated.

## Florida
- **Agency:** Florida Department of Commerce / Reemployment Assistance (formerly DEO)
- **File:** https://connect.myflorida.com/
- **Phone:** 1-833-352-7759
- **Waiting period:** 1 wk
